Research Computing's new website is going live on Wednesday, June 10. You will still find us at rc.virginia.edu, but the site's look and navigation will be improved to better serve our users and the research community at UVA.

If you have specific pages bookmarked, you may need to update them to the new URL once the site launches. You can navigate around the site using the main navigation, search, or the new "I Want To" menu with quick links.

If you still need help, the User Support team will be on standby to assist. On the new site, you can find the User Support team under "Contact Us" and Support.
